1.Overview

This report is a picture of cash going in and out of one fictional site as it moves from Bitcoin mining to a data center. The numbers are examples, not a live company feed. Finance already has cash, bills, and loans; operations has how much Bitcoin the site still produces; commercial has when the data center should open and what it might earn; planning has “what if” assumptions like Bitcoin price and construction delay. Those pieces do not arrive in one language, so we first tidy them so months and dollars line up, then name them the way a cash-flow statement does (day-to-day operations, building the facility, money from lenders). Only after that do we assemble the Forecast tab — the chart and table you can click through. Changing Bitcoin price, cost overrun, or delay on that tab is like asking “what if?” without touching the original source files.

3.Data Model

The transform script types amounts as USD numbers, maps calendar dates to YYYY-MM and a forecast month index (October 2026 = 1), and writes one table per entity. Grain is site × forecast month unless noted.

4.Semantic Layer

Business names and driver math sit here — not in the source systems. The Forecast tab’s Operating / Investing / Financing rows and the three scenario knobs are this layer. Category labels and colors are stored on the reporting mart because they are presentation for this report, not ERP fields.

Category Inflow driver Outflow driver
Operating Activities Bitcoin sales + data center customer payments Mining costs + other fixed operating costs
Investing Activities Not modeled (always $0) Construction payments × (1 + overrun %), shifted by delay
Financing Activities Debt draws + equity funding Not modeled (always $0)

Bitcoin sales[m] = BTC produced[m] × BTC price. Mining costs[m] = BTC produced[m] × power cost per BTC + other mining opex. Net cash flow = total inflow − total outflow. Ending cash = beginning cash + net. Editable in the UI: BTC price, construction cost overrun %, construction delay. Everything else is a fixed assumption.

5.Reporting

The reporting tier is a single mart, data/reporting/cashflow-forecast.json, shaped for this dashboard only: forecast, defaultInputs, fixedAssumptions, mining, construction, dataCenter, otherFixedOpexMonthly, cashFlowCategories. The Forecast tab fetches that file, recalculates on input change, and renders stacked O/I/F bars, a net cash-flow line, and the monthly detail table.
